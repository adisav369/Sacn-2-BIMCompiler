## Concept

CHANGE(element_filter, new_product_id) replaces existing wall products with a different
library item. This is Level 2 (PRODUCT_SWAP) on the Type Resolution Ladder — the new
product is in the library, the position stays fixed. No geometry inference. No variance.

The operation:
1. Identifies IfcWall elements matching element_filter (storey, ifc_class, current product)
2. Creates W_BOM_Variance (gesture_type=PANEL, resolved_type=PRODUCT_SWAP, resolution_level=2)
3. verb_sequence = "SWAP ROOM <parent_bom> <line_id> PRODUCT <new_product_id>"
4. Compiler re-runs → new wall geometry from new product dimensions
5. On promote (AP): C_OrderLine.M_Product_ID updated
