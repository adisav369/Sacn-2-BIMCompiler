HRD Corp Training
HRDC-claimable technology workshops for cooperatives and SMEs. Entruss Ventures is a certified training provider — these programmes can be delivered under the existing HRD Corp levy claim framework.
Featured Programme
1-Day Technology Update Workshop:
AI Impact & Preparedness
For cooperative board members, managers, and technical staff. HRDC-claimable under employer levy. Delivered by Entruss Ventures.
09:00 – 10:30
Module 1: AI Landscape
What is AI today? From ChatGPT to industry-specific AI. Demystifying the technology for non-technical leaders. Real examples from cooperatives and SMEs.
10:45 – 12:15
Module 2: Impact on Your Sector
How AI is changing construction, housing, agriculture, and financial services. Cooperative-specific opportunities and risks. Case study: BIM Compiler for affordable housing.
13:30 – 15:00
Module 3: Preparing Your Organisation
Digital readiness assessment. Low-cost first steps. Open-source tools vs proprietary. Building internal capability without breaking the budget.
15:15 – 17:00
Module 4: Hands-On & Action Plan
Live demo: AI tools for document processing, cost estimation, and design. Each participant drafts a 90-day AI readiness action plan for their cooperative.
