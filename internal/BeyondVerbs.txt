The Decision Model and Notation standard (DMN) is used to model repeatable business decisions . Examples include:
- "Decide on the level of discount to offer a customer"
- "Decide when a stocked item should be ordered"

**Why DMN matters:** It formalizes what you're doing intuitively—turning business decisions into **visible, model-able components** that can be verified and reused.

### The Academic Foundation

This is **exactly** what your verb contracts (preconditions/postconditions) would enable.
