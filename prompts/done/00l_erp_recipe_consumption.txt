## Architecture constraint (read before diagnosing)

**MEP_RECIPE is abstract.** A recipe archetype describes a PATTERN (standoff, chain
geometry), not a per-instance record. It is reusable across all scenarios.
Validation Rules (DV rules) are what determine the final expression of a recipe
for a specific building — the compilation must NOT explode archetypes into per-instance
LEAF rows. The correct compiled output for a discipline is ONE DISCIPLINE row (the scope
declaration) and its LEAF children from the *_SYSTEM BOM (abstract scope items such as
FP_RISER, FP_SPRINKLER_LAYOUT). The MEP_RECIPE count in ERP.db drives the Qty on the
DISCIPLINE row — NOT the LEAF row structure.
